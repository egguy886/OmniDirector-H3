# Sub-Skill 01: Screenplay Dramaturgy & Momentum Decoupling

## Purpose
Traditional screenplays written for live-action human actors are inherently incompatible with generative diffusion video models (such as MiniMax H3). Humans can perform compound, concurrent actions (e.g. "smiling while turning his head, opening the bottle, and looking anxiously at the door"). In diffusion models, such descriptions cause immediate latent collapse, warping limbs, morphing objects, and facial distortion.

This sub-skill provides two standardized production formats—**Mode A (Domestic Chinese)** and **Mode B (Global Streaming)**—and enforces **Atomic Momentum Decoupling** to author rock-solid, production-ready screenplays optimized for MiniMax H3.

## The Dual Production Modes (Mode A & Mode B)

| 特性 / Mode | Mode A：国内中文微短剧标准 | Mode B：出海微短剧双模标准 |
|---|---|---|
| **发行平台** | 抖音、快手、微信视频号、番茄短剧、红果短剧、微视 | ReelShort、DramaBox、ShortMax、TikTok、ShortWave |
| **动作调度 (`△`)** | 全中文精确白描（高动量、高密度因果动词） | 全中文精确白描（与国内标准相同，供 AI 空间解耦） |
| **角色命名** | 地道中文角色名（如：林舟、陆辰、苏晴） | 国际化英文角色名（如：Cade, Reeve, Nia） |
| **对白语言** | 地道中文口语，节奏紧凑、短促有力、强化即时反转 | 纯正美式/国际英语，口语化、含蓄、带停顿与潜台词 |
| **潜台词系统** | 可选标注角色心理动机与反转线索 | 显式标注 `[中文潜台词: ...]` 供主创团队审核 |
| **文化与审查适配** | 深度符合国内广电与短视频平台网络视听内容审核标准 | 符合出海平台快节奏、强设定、高情绪浓缩规范 |

## Core Methodologies

### 1. Atomic Momentum Decoupling (原子动力学解耦)
- **One Kinetic Vector per Beat**: Never assign more than one major physical motion to an actor within a single shot beat.
- **Causal Triad**: Every physical interaction must obey the sequence:
  $$\text{接触 (Contact)} \longrightarrow \text{物理受力 (Reaction)} \longrightarrow \text{稳态/释放 (Steady State)}$$
- **Zero Compound Action Rule**:
  - ❌ *Incorrect*: "张明疾步跑向桌子，抓起合同，一边瞪着李总一边愤怒地质问。"
  - ✅ *Correct (Mode A)*:
    - `△ 张明疾步冲至铁皮长桌边缘，左手掌心重重按在桌面冷金属上。` (Beat 1 - 驻足与锚定)
    - `△ 他的右手手指扣住牛皮纸合同边缘，向后猛然拖动半寸。` (Beat 2 - 接触与位移)
    - `△ 张明胸口起伏，抬眸盯住对面三米处的李总。` (Beat 3 - 视线与对峙)

### 2. Implicit Physiological & Emotional Tension (生理体感白描防审查)
- **Sensory Realism over Generic Adjectives**: Replace subjective emotional labels (*"he was deeply turned on"* / *"性感惹火"*) with observable physical phenomena:
  - 锁骨凹陷处的汗珠折射冷光、前臂紧绷时浮现的肌腱与青筋；
  - 滚烫的气流在 4°C 零度晨雾中凝结为白气；
  - 距离缩减至极限 10 公分；指尖在距离手腕一毫米处微颤悬停。
- **Safe-by-Design Phrasing**: Pass all automated AI safety filters without triggering censorship rejections while guiding diffusion models to generate palpable athletic and romantic tension.

### 3. 15s/120s Micro-Drama Hook Architecture
- Each episode is nominally 120 seconds, subdivided into 8 discrete 15.0-second production segments (H3-01 through H3-08).
- **Hook Rules**:
  - **00s-03s (黄金前置钩子)**: Immediate visual disruption or physical confrontation (e.g. gate slamming shut, red warning light, sudden grab).
  - **15s/30s (微反转与阵营变动)**: Sudden shift of physical leverage or discovery of key evidence.
  - **105s-120s (单集断章悬崖 Cliffhanger)**: Unresolved spatial or informational crisis forcing immediate next-episode swipe.
