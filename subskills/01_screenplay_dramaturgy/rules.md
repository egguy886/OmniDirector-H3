# Dramaturgy Rules & Cheat Sheet

## 1. Action Decoupling Checklist

| Phase | Duration | Permitted Actions | AI Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Approach / Setup** | 0.0s – 3.5s | Single directional trajectory, footfall, camera push | Low |
| **Contact / Climax** | 3.5s – 8.0s | Hand contact, prop grip, gaze snap, single spoken line | Medium |
| **Reaction / Lock** | 8.0s – 12.0s | Breath heave, recoil, second character facial micro-reaction | Low |
| **Steady State / Tail** | 12.0s – 15.0s | Decelerate all motion; camera locks on steady anchor | Zero (Essential for Tail Relay) |

## 2. Physiological Phrasing Lexicon (Safety-Compliant)

| Intended Feeling | Banned / Risky Triggers | Compliant High-Tension Phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| Athletic Physique | Sexy body, naked, ripped, erotic | Lean athletic definition under sweat-dampened cotton training jersey; sharp shoulder contour; veins tracing along forearm tendons |
| Intimacy / Heat | Seductive, passionate kiss, bed scene | Controlled proximity (10 cm distance); shallow rapid chest heaves; warm breath clouding the cold air; fingertips hovering a millimeter above bare wrist |
| Violent Conflict | Brutal beating, bloody gore, choke | Sudden kinetic collar grab; fabric tension pulling taut; knuckles whitening; back slamming against concrete pillar with dust particle vibration |
| Restraint / Secrecy | Sneaky, guilty | Glancing sideways without turning skull; index finger sliding a bronze key beneath an iron plate; thumb pressing against jawline to suppress speech |
