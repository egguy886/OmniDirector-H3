# OmniDirector-H3: Industrial Screenplay-to-Video Engine for MiniMax H3

**OmniDirector-H3 (全知导演 H3)** is an enterprise-grade AI cinematic pipeline specifically designed and optimized for **MiniMax H3 (MiniMax's flagship video & audio multimodal foundation model)**. 

It bridges the critical gap between literary screenwriting and AI video diffusion generation, transforming narrative intentions into high-fidelity, continuity-locked 15-second micro-drama video segments.

## 💡 What Does "Omni" Mean? / 为什么叫 Omni？

**"Omni"** (/ˈɒmni/) originates from the Latin *omnis*, signifying **"all", "universal", "all-encompassing", or "all-knowing"** (as seen in *Omniscient* [全知], *Omnipotent* [全能], and NVIDIA *Omniverse* [全宇宙/全维度虚拟世界]).

1. **Omni-Dimensional (全模态统筹)**: Unifying narrative literature, 3D spatial geometry, 24fps physical kinetics, facial micro-expressions, and acoustic Foley into one coherent specification.
2. **Omni-Market (全场景兼容)**: Natively supporting both **Mode A (Domestic Chinese Micro-Dramas)** and **Mode B (Global Overseas Streaming)**.
3. **Omni-Consistent (全周期连续)**: Enforcing strict 180° camera axes, prop damage lifecycles, and `TAIL_RELAY` steady-state inheritance across multi-episode productions.

## 🎯 Target Model: MiniMax H3

This system is built from the ground up to conform to the native architecture and cross-attention mechanics of **MiniMax H3**:
* **Native Multimodal Dual-Stream**: Harmonizes visual frame diffusion with synchronous acoustic/dialogue generation.
* **Official Timestamped Cutting Protocol**: Leverages MiniMax H3's native ability to execute hard shot cuts at millisecond timestamps (`[Shot 2] At 00:03.500...`) within a single continuous 15-second generation.
* **Official Vocal Synthesis Tags**: Formats all spoken dialogue into strict `<d> Speaker: "..." </d>` blocks.
* **Acoustic Stems Isolation**: Enforces `non_diegetic_music: SILENT` to ensure pristine voice and Foley stems, leaving music scoring to post-production.

## 🎨 Asset Generation Policies (视觉与音频资产政策)

### 1. Visual Assets (视觉资产)
* 🌟 **Primary Recommended**: Invoke **`imagegem`** inside **Codex** for character 3-view sheets, 45° profile shots, and stable cinematic lighting.
* 🥈 **Secondary Recommended**: Use **`nanobanana`** for high-concept empty backgrounds and complex prop textures.
* Standards: Characters need neutral micro-expression front + 45° turn + locked wardrobe; scenes must be strictly empty plates.

### 2. Audio Assets (音频资产)
* 🔇 **No Generator Recommended (Open Choice)**: The pipeline strictly avoids tool bias. The user may supply recordings from human voice actors, open-source models (VoxCPM, CosyVoice, GPT-SoVITS, F5-TTS), or commercial TTS (ElevenLabs).
* Standards: Lossless **32kHz Mono WAV**, pure dry voice (no BGM/reverb), 5-15s duration.
* Hard Rule: All prompt segments must enforce `non_diegetic_music: SILENT`.

## 🚀 Execution Freedom / 用户自主选择运行环境

OmniDirector-H3's core responsibility is **compiling narrative intent into flawless, 0-error MiniMax H3 multimodal prompts and continuity matrices**. 

Once compiled, creators have 100% freedom in choosing how to execute the prompts with MiniMax H3:
* **Option 1: MiniMax Official Web Platform**: Copy and paste the verified 15s prompt units directly into MiniMax's creative studio interface.
* **Option 2: MiniMax Official Open Platform API**: Integrate the compiled prompts into private enterprise production systems using official REST APIs.
* **Option 3: Cloud GPU ComfyUI Nodes**: Deploy via AutoDL, RunPod, or local studio clusters using ComfyUI's MiniMax H3 workflow nodes.
* **Option 4: Automated Batch Tool**: Use the reference batch runner provided in `tools/run_overnight_batch.py` for unattended overnight rendering.
